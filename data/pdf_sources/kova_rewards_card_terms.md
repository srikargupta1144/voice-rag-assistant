# Kova Rewards Visa Card: Pricing and Terms

Sample document. Kova Bank is a fictional bank created for this demonstration. Nothing in this document describes a real credit card.

## Card Overview and Rewards

The Kova Rewards Visa card earns 1.5 percent cash back on all eligible purchases. Grocery store purchases earn 3 percent cash back on up to 6,000 dollars of grocery spending each calendar year, after which they earn 1.5 percent. There is no annual fee for the Kova Rewards Visa card.

Cash back can be redeemed as a statement credit or a deposit into a Kova checking or savings account. The minimum redemption is 25 dollars. Rewards do not expire while the account is open and in good standing, but unredeemed rewards are forfeited if the account is closed because of fraud or abuse.

## Interest Rates

The purchase APR is a variable rate between 19.99 percent and 27.99 percent, based on your creditworthiness. New cardholders receive an introductory APR of 0 percent on purchases for the first 15 months after account opening.

Balance transfers made within 60 days of account opening receive an introductory APR of 0 percent for 12 months. After the introductory period, the purchase APR applies to any remaining transferred balance. The cash advance APR is 29.49 percent, variable. Kova does not charge a penalty APR.

## Fees

The balance transfer fee is 3 percent of each transfer, with a minimum of 5 dollars. The cash advance fee is 5 percent of each advance, with a minimum of 10 dollars. There is no foreign transaction fee.

The late payment fee is up to 30 dollars for the first late payment and up to 41 dollars if another payment is late within the next six billing cycles. The returned payment fee is up to 25 dollars. Kova does not charge an over-the-limit fee.

## Payments and Grace Period

Your due date is at least 25 days after the close of each billing cycle. Kova will not charge interest on purchases if you pay your entire balance by the due date each month. This interest-free period is called the grace period.

The minimum payment is the greater of 35 dollars or 1 percent of the new balance plus interest and fees, or the full balance if it is less than 35 dollars. You can set up automatic payments for the minimum payment, the statement balance, or a fixed amount in the Kova mobile app.
