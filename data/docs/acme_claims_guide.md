# Acme Mutual Home Claims Guide

Sample document. Acme Mutual Insurance Company is a fictional company created for this demonstration.

## Reporting a claim

Report a loss as soon as possible, ideally within 72 hours. You must report a claim no later than 60 days after the date of loss, or Acme may deny it. You can report a claim in the Acme app, online at acme.example/claims, or by phone at the fictional claims number 1-800-555-0142, which is open 24 hours a day.

When you report a claim you receive a claim number. Keep it handy, because every message about the claim uses it.

## What to do right after a loss

First make sure everyone is safe. Then take reasonable steps to prevent more damage, for example covering a broken window or shutting off the water. Acme reimburses reasonable emergency repairs up to 2,500 dollars without prior approval, so keep every receipt. Do not throw away damaged items until the adjuster has seen them or told you that photos are enough.

If your home cannot be lived in, keep receipts for hotel stays, meals and other extra costs so they can be paid under Coverage D.

## Documents you will need

Take photos or videos of all damage before you clean up. Make a list of damaged or stolen items with their approximate age and value. For a theft, you must file a police report within 48 hours of discovering the theft and give Acme the report number.

## The adjuster visit

Acme assigns an adjuster within 2 business days after you report a claim. The adjuster contacts you to schedule an inspection, which usually takes place within 7 days. For small claims under 5,000 dollars, Acme may settle the claim from photos without an inspection, through the Fast Track process.

## How claims are paid

Acme makes a coverage decision within 30 days after it receives your signed proof of loss. Once you and Acme agree on the amount, payment is sent within 10 business days by direct deposit or check. Payments for a mortgaged home include your mortgage company as a payee.

For dwelling losses settled at replacement cost, Acme first pays the actual cash value. The remaining amount, called recoverable depreciation, is paid once repairs are completed, as long as they are completed within 180 days of the first payment.

## Disagreeing with a decision

If you disagree with the amount of a loss, you can ask for appraisal in writing within 60 days of the claim decision. You and Acme each choose an independent appraiser, and the two appraisers choose an umpire. A written agreement by any two of the three sets the amount of the loss. You pay your own appraiser, and the umpire's cost is shared equally.
