# Acme Mutual Home Insurance Policy (Form AH-3)

Sample document. Acme Mutual Insurance Company is a fictional company created for this demonstration. Nothing in this document describes a real insurance product.

## About this policy

This policy is a contract between you, the named insured shown in the Declarations, and Acme Mutual Insurance Company. The policy period is twelve months and starts at 12:01 a.m. on the effective date shown in the Declarations. The policy renews automatically for another twelve months unless you or Acme decide not to renew it. Acme will mail a renewal offer at least 45 days before the policy period ends.

The policy is made up of the Declarations page, this policy form, and any endorsements you have chosen. If an endorsement conflicts with this form, the endorsement applies.

## Coverage A: Dwelling and Coverage B: Other Structures

Coverage A pays to repair or rebuild your house, including attached structures such as an attached garage, deck or porch. Losses to the dwelling are settled at replacement cost, which means the cost to repair or replace with materials of like kind and quality, without a deduction for depreciation, once the repair is actually completed. The most Acme pays is the Coverage A limit shown in the Declarations.

Coverage B pays for structures on your property that are not attached to the house, such as a detached garage, a fence or a garden shed. The Coverage B limit is 10 percent of the Coverage A limit. Structures used for business purposes are not covered under Coverage B.

## Coverage C: Personal Property and Coverage D: Loss of Use

Coverage C pays for personal property you own or use anywhere in the world, such as furniture, clothing and electronics. The Coverage C limit is 50 percent of the Coverage A limit. Personal property is settled at actual cash value unless you buy the Replacement Cost Contents endorsement HE-07.

Special limits apply to some types of property for any one loss: cash and gift cards up to 200 dollars, theft of jewelry and watches up to 1,500 dollars, theft of firearms up to 2,500 dollars, and business property kept at home up to 2,500 dollars.

Coverage D pays additional living expenses, such as hotel bills and extra meal costs, when a covered loss makes your home unfit to live in. The Coverage D limit is 20 percent of the Coverage A limit, and Acme pays these expenses for the shortest time needed to repair the home, up to a maximum of 12 months.

## Exclusions

Acme does not pay for loss caused by any of the following: flood, surface water or waves; earthquake or earth movement; wear and tear, rust or gradual deterioration; damage by insects, rodents or birds; intentional loss caused by an insured person; and war or nuclear hazard.

Water that backs up through sewers or drains is excluded unless you add the Water Backup endorsement HE-12, which provides up to 10,000 dollars per loss. Mold is excluded, except that Acme pays up to 5,000 dollars for mold removal when the mold results from a covered water loss.

## Deductibles

A deductible is the amount of each covered loss that you pay before Acme pays. The standard all-peril deductible is 1,000 dollars. You may choose a 500 dollar or a 2,500 dollar deductible instead, which changes your premium.

Losses caused by windstorm or hail have a separate deductible equal to 2 percent of the Coverage A limit. The deductible applies once per occurrence, even if more than one coverage part is involved in the same loss.

## Premium Payments and Cancellation

You can pay the premium annually, in two semi-annual payments, or in monthly installments. Each monthly installment carries a 3 dollar service fee. If a payment is late, there is a grace period of 15 days before Acme sends a notice of cancellation.

You may cancel this policy at any time by contacting Acme. If you cancel, Acme refunds any unearned premium on a pro rata basis, with no cancellation fee. If Acme cancels for nonpayment of premium, it will give you at least 10 days written notice. If Acme cancels for any other allowed reason, it will give at least 30 days notice. After the policy has been in force for 60 days, Acme may cancel only for nonpayment, fraud, or a substantial change in the risk.

## Discounts

The following discounts may reduce your premium. A multi-policy discount of 12 percent applies when you also insure a vehicle with Acme. A protective device discount of 5 percent applies when the home has a centrally monitored burglar or fire alarm. A claims-free discount of 8 percent applies when you have had no claims in the last 3 years. A new roof discount of 4 percent applies when the roof is less than 10 years old.
