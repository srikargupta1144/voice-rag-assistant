# Kova Bank Account FAQ

Sample document. Kova Bank is a fictional bank created for this demonstration. Nothing in this document describes a real bank account.

## How do I open an account?

You can open a Kova account in the Kova mobile app or at kova.example in about ten minutes. You must be at least 18 years old and have a valid government-issued photo ID and a residential address. The minimum opening deposit is 25 dollars for Everyday Checking and 100 dollars for High Yield Savings. Joint accounts can be opened with up to two owners.

## Is there a monthly fee on Everyday Checking?

Everyday Checking has a monthly maintenance fee of 8 dollars. The fee is waived for any statement cycle in which you keep a daily balance of at least 1,500 dollars or receive at least 500 dollars in direct deposits. The fee is also waived for students under 25 years old.

## What happens if I spend more than my balance?

Kova does not charge overdraft fees on Everyday Checking. If a debit card purchase or ATM withdrawal would overdraw your account, it is declined. You can turn on free Overdraft Transfer, which moves money from your linked Kova savings account to cover a transaction.

## How much interest does High Yield Savings earn?

High Yield Savings currently earns 3.80 percent APY. The rate is variable and may change at any time. Interest is compounded daily and credited to your account monthly. You can make 6 free withdrawals or transfers out of savings per statement cycle, and each additional withdrawal costs 5 dollars.

## How long do transfers and wires take?

Standard ACH transfers to other banks arrive in 1 to 3 business days. Transfers to other Kova customers are instant and free. Outgoing domestic wires cost 25 dollars and outgoing international wires cost 45 dollars. Incoming wires are free. Wires requested before 3 p.m. Eastern time on a business day are sent the same day.

## What are the mobile check deposit limits?

You can deposit up to 5,000 dollars per day and up to 10,000 dollars in any 30-day period with mobile check deposit. The first 225 dollars of a mobile deposit is available on the next business day, and the rest is usually available within 2 business days. Keep the paper check for 14 days after the deposit, then destroy it.

## What should I do if my card is lost or stolen?

Freeze your card right away in the Kova app, then report it as lost or stolen in the app or by calling the number on the back of the card. A replacement card arrives in 5 to 7 business days, and expedited delivery in 2 business days is free. If you report an unauthorized debit card transaction within 2 business days, you are not responsible for it under the Kova zero liability promise.

## How do I close my account?

You can close an account at any time in the app or by calling Kova. There is no fee to close an account, except that accounts closed within 90 days of opening are charged a 25 dollar early closure fee. Any remaining balance is sent to you by transfer or by mailed check.

## How can I contact Kova?

Kova customer support is available by phone every day from 7 a.m. to 11 p.m. Eastern time, and by chat in the app 24 hours a day. The fictional support number for this sample is 1-800-555-0199.
